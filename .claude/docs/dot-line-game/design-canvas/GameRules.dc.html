How to play

1Take turns drawing a line between two neighboring dots: left, right, up or down.
2The first line can go anywhere. After that, start from a dot that already has a line.
3Draw the fourth side of a square to claim it. One line can claim two squares.
4After every line, the turn passes to the other player.
5The game ends when all squares are claimed or someone presses Stop. Most squares wins; equal scores are a draw.
