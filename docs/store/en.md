## Full description (1364 / 4000)

```text
Dots and Boxes is the classic pencil-and-paper game, made for a phone or tablet.

HOW TO PLAY
Take turns drawing a line between two neighboring dots. Draw the fourth side of a square and the square is yours. One line can close two squares. When every square is taken, whoever owns the most squares wins. Equal scores make a draw.

THE CONNECTED RULE
The first line can go anywhere. Every next line must start from a dot that a line already reaches. Your lines grow out of one connected chain, so every move counts and the board never falls apart into separate corners.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Versus computer: Easy picks a random line. Normal claims squares whenever it can and tries not to give any away.
• Two players: pass one phone or tablet between two people.
• Board size from 2×2 to 10×10 squares: a quick game on the bus or a long one on the couch.
• Pick your color, then play a Rematch with one tap.
• Stop a game at any time: the current score decides the result.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• Clear "How to play" rules with pictures, one tap away.
• Light and dark themes.
• English and Ukrainian.
• Works fully offline, from the first launch.
• Your game is saved on the device, so you can pick up where you left off.

NO ADS. NO DATA.
No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. The app collects no data at all: games and settings stay on your device.
```
